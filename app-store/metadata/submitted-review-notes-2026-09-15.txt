Access and setup
- Use the active demo account supplied in the sign-in fields. No OTP, invitation, sample file or special hardware is required for that account. Apple, Google and email sign-in are available. Accept updated required terms if prompted.
- Candidate uses https://api.ghkdqhrbals.org and Apple sandbox purchases in TestFlight.

Purpose and main flow
- For learners age 14+, BuddyStudy generates recall questions, grades answers and saves learning records by topic and difficulty.
- Home > My Studies > open/create study or topic > generate question > answer > submit. Grading is asynchronous. View saved answers and feedback in Records; Statistics presents topic progress.
- Study/topic creation does not consume question allowance. Active answer drafts are retained.

Accounts, community and permissions
- Settings > Account Settings > Delete Account. Please use a disposable account for deletion, not the permanent demo account.
- Home > All Studies: public questions/comments. Signed-in users can like/comment. Question menu (...) > Report Question or Block User. Long-press a comment to block its author. Blocking hides that author's content.
- Profile > Notification Settings controls notifications. Existing permission was already set on the recorded device. Microphone access is used for Buddy voice conversations; no camera, location, contacts, photos or ATT prompt is needed.

Membership and purchases
- Home > top-left Profile > Membership & Billing > Manage membership.
- Free: 30 questions per monthly allowance period; Buddy voice unavailable.
- Plus: monthly auto-renewable, 300 questions and 60 voice minutes. Pro: monthly auto-renewable, 1,000 questions and 60 voice minutes. Both remove public-feed ads.
- Korean storefront regular monthly prices: Plus KRW 19,900; Pro KRW 39,900. Eligible new subscribers receive the first month at KRW 9,900 / 19,900 respectively. Apple determines introductory eligibility; the system purchase sheet is authoritative.
- Membership shows price, duration, benefits, renewal disclosure, Terms, Privacy, Restore and purchase/change controls. Select Plus/Pro to open Apple's sheet; the recording cancels without purchasing.
