BuddyStudy 1.1.0 review guide

Sign-in
- Use the demo account configured in App Review Information.
- The review account is already active and does not require creating a new account.

Primary study flow
1. Open Home and select My Studies.
2. Open an existing study or add a topic.
3. Create a question, enter an answer, and submit it.
4. AI grading is processed asynchronously. The page updates without replacing the active answer draft.
5. Open Records or Statistics to review the saved result and topic-level progress.

Public community and moderation
- Open Home > All Studies to browse public answered questions.
- A signed-in reviewer can like, comment, or report a public question.
- To block an abusive user, long-press a public question and choose Block user. The same action is available from the ellipsis menu on the question detail page and by long-pressing a comment.
- Blocked users' public questions and comments are hidden immediately.

Account and privacy
- Account deletion is available in Settings > Account Settings > Delete Account.
- Privacy policy: https://ghkdqhrbals.github.io/buddy-studdy/en/privacy.html
- Terms: https://ghkdqhrbals.github.io/buddy-studdy/en/terms.html

Localization and notifications
- App UI and content display support Korean, English, and Japanese.
- Translated content provides a Show original / View translation control.
- Notification taps open the related question or announcement. Push delivery itself is not required to exercise the core review flow.

No purchases, subscriptions, advertisements, or IDFA tracking are included.
