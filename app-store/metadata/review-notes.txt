Purpose and main flow
- For learners age 14+, BuddyStudy creates active-recall questions, grades answers, saves records, and shows topic progress.
1. Home > My Studies > open/create a study or topic > generate a question > answer > submit.
2. Grading completes asynchronously without replacing an active answer draft.
3. Records and Statistics show the saved answer, AI feedback, and topic progress.

Accounts, UGC, and permissions
- Delete account: Settings > Account Settings > Delete Account. Use a disposable account, not this permanent review account.
- Home > All Studies shows public questions/comments. A signed-in user can like/comment, choose ... > Report Question, or Block User. Blocking hides that author's questions/comments; long-press a comment to block its author.
- Profile > Notification Settings requests notification permission contextually. No location, contacts, camera, photos, or ATT permission is requested.

In-App Purchase
- Path: Home > top-left Profile > Membership & Billing > Manage membership.
- Tier 2: auto-renewable monthly, 300 AI questions. Tier 3: auto-renewable monthly, 1,000. Both remove public-feed ads. Each generated question uses one allowance; study/topic creation does not.
- Offers show title, localized price, one-month duration, allowance, renewal disclosure, Terms, Privacy, Restore, and purchase/change control. Get Tier/Switch to Tier opens Apple's purchase sheet; cancel before purchase. This free account shows purchase/restore; Manage Subscription requires an active subscription.

External services
- AWS Seoul: API, data, operator-managed LibreTranslate. Cloudflare: DNS, proxy, TLS, security. OpenAI: questions, grading, feedback, suggestions, translation fallback.
- Apple: sign-in, billing, APNs. RevenueCat: subscription purchase/sync. Google: sign-in, email verification, Firebase, UMP, and AdMob. Sentry: masked diagnostics.

Regions and status
- Features are consistent across regions and localized in Korean, English, and Japanese; storefront price, currency, and tax vary.
- Not regulated and no protected third-party material: this is a general educational aid, not a medical, financial, gambling, legal, or other regulated service. It has no licensed media catalog. Questions/comments are UGC; Terms prohibit infringement, and reporting/blocking are available.
- Advertising: the free account may receive one server-positioned native ad on Home > All Studies after two questions. AdMob is tried first; a BuddyStudy/Coupang campaign is fallback only.
