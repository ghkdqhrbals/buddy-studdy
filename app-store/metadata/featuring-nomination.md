## Description

BuddyStudy helps curious learners explain what they know instead of only reading
an answer. A learner chooses a topic, answers a short question in their own
words, and receives AI feedback with an explanation. A branching study tree
connects broad interests to smaller topics, while topic-level statistics show
where understanding is developing and where more practice may help.

The next update simplifies discovery into a public feed with search and topic
following. Readers can explore completed public questions without creating an
account, and choose topics to follow after signing in. An occasional editorial
notification can take a learner directly to a selected public question or an
announcement. Incoming content preserves an unfinished answer draft.

This update focuses on a clear path from discovering a useful question to
maintaining a personal learning practice. The interface and study content
support Korean, English and Japanese. Public content includes reporting and
blocking controls. Free access and optional monthly memberships remain clearly
distinguished from free topic following.
