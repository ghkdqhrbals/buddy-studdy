## Description for the editorial team

BuddyStudy helps learners move from a broad subject to a question they can
answer in their own words. A topic tree organizes that journey; short questions,
AI feedback and saved answers give each branch a learning history.

Version 1.2.0 adds a way to find the next question: learners follow their
interests and explore public questions ranked by topic relevance, views,
likes and freshness. First-study suggestions open an editable topic form,
and public question links let another learner start from the same prompt.
Creating topics and following interests do not consume the question allowance.

The update is designed to connect discovery with active study. Draft answers
remain protected when new questions arrive. Progress stays specific to the
topic, so learners can see what to revisit. The app supports Korean, English
and Japanese and includes Voice Tutor conversations with saved learning records.
