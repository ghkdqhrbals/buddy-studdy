Main flow
- Home > My Studies > create/open a study or topic > generate a question > answer > submit. Grading is asynchronous; saved drafts are preserved. Records and Statistics show learning history and topic progress.
- On an empty My Studies screen, starter topics prefill an editable study form. The user must save explicitly. Selecting a starter, creating studies/topics, and following interests do not generate questions or consume question allowance.

Discovery and sharing
- Home > All Studies defaults to Recommended: followed topics first, then views, likes and freshness. Signed-in users can save up to 30 interests, browse Following, or sort by newest, views or likes. Interests sync per account; guests receive general recommendations.
- Public-question menus offer topic follow/unfollow and eligible question links. Only completed public questions and eligible completed voice exchanges from the production API can be shared.
- https://api.ghkdqhrbals.org/questions/{numericId}?tl=en opens the same public record in a supported installed app. Without the app, the landing shows only topic/question text and an App Store link. After installation, reopen the original link. Private/deleted/unavailable records return a generic 404; web previews omit answers, scores, authors and audio.
- For another user's public content, use the question menu to Report Question or Block User; long-press a comment to block its author. Own records have Make private/Delete actions. Profile provides Liked Questions. Account deletion is in Settings > Account Settings.

Voice and membership
- The phone button beside Home Search starts Voice Tutor when signed in and server allowance permits. Free has 30 monthly questions and no voice access; Plus has 300 questions and Pro 1,000. Each paid tier includes a separate 60-minute monthly voice allowance and an ad-free public feed.
- Voice uses the microphone. Recording requires a separate per-call choice. Publishing an eligible learning exchange does not publish the full session transcript or private recording.
- Profile > Membership & Billing > Manage membership shows localized monthly prices, renewal terms, Restore, Terms and Privacy. Apple determines introductory eligibility. Open the system purchase sheet and cancel for a purchase-presentation check; do not assume every account qualifies for an offer.
- Free public feeds may show non-personalized AdMob native ads, with BuddyStudy/Coupang fallback. No ATT/IDFA tracking is used.

Rating requests
- Apple's native review request may be attempted only after a new visible grading completion, at least three learning days and seven days since the first completion, then returning to idle Home. No score/satisfaction screening, reward or feature unlock is involved. StoreKit may not display a prompt; a review is never required.
