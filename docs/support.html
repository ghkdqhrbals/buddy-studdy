Connecting your account

Connect BuddyStudy from ChatGPT, then choose Apple, Google or email on the BuddyStudy login page. Use the same provider account used in the iOS app. Social login does not create an account or link different accounts by email.

For email login, enter the email shown in your BuddyStudy profile and the eight-digit code sent to it. Email-registered accounts can instead use their existing password. Review any pending required account terms and the separate requested ChatGPT access before connecting.

Profile access and older connections

Profile, saved resume and interest reads require separate profile:read consent. An older connection cannot gain this permission by refreshing its token. Reconnect BuddyStudy and review the requested access when you want to use profile information in ChatGPT.

Login and permission errors

Start the connection again in ChatGPT if the ten-minute login request expires. If email and password are rejected, verify that the account was registered with email; use the original Apple or Google provider for social accounts. Manage your account in the BuddyStudy app, or contact support for help.

When contacting support, include the time and visible error message. Never send your password, API key, authentication code or access token.

Disconnecting

Open connection management to stop future access. Content already shared in ChatGPT remains in that conversation and must be managed in ChatGPT.
