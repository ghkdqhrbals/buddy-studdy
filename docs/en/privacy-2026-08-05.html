BuddyStudy

Privacy Policy

Published and effective: August 5, 2026

BuddyStudy processes only the data needed for learning and account features and does not sell personal data. MySQL in the AWS Seoul region is the source of truth for learning data. Settings, drafts, caches, and diagnostic logs may also be stored on the device.

1. Controller

The BuddyStudy Operator (the “Operator”) controls personal data processed by the BuddyStudy iOS app, APIs, notifications, public questions, and related web pages.

Public questions can be read without signing in. Personal learning records, statistics, comments, likes, profiles, and notification synchronization require an account and device registration.

2. Purposes

  • Email, Google, or Apple sign-in, device registration, sessions, and account security
  • Study trees, question generation, drafts, AI grading and feedback, translations, records, and statistics
  • Question and comment notifications and optional marketing notifications
  • Public questions, comments, likes, reports, feedback, and profile features
  • Quota, terms acceptance, and notification preference management
  • App Store subscription purchase, restore, change, cancellation and refund guidance, membership fulfillment, and billing history
  • Reliability, security, abuse prevention, quality, and performance improvement

The Service does not intentionally collect government identifiers, health data, biometrics, or other sensitive data. Do not enter such data into study answers or comments.

Apple processes payment card numbers, bank-account details, and Apple ID payment credentials. BuddyStudy and RevenueCat do not directly collect or store those payment credentials.

Data is collected through app input, identity providers, API requests, APNs registration, and SDK automatic collection. Passwords, verification codes, access and Google ID tokens, APNs authentication keys, and client secrets are masked in logs.

4. Public content

If sharing is enabled, the question, topic, difficulty, creation and answer times, score, views, likes, comment count, and public profile may be visible to others. Public comments and their authors' public profiles are also visible.

Drafts, private answers, email addresses, credentials, APNs tokens, device IDs, and internal logs are not public.

7. Analytics and diagnostics

The release app uses Google Analytics for Firebase for coarse screen and feature events and Firebase Remote Config to retrieve app-control settings such as maintenance and update policies. BuddyStudy does not put user IDs, email, study topics, questions, answers, comments, tokens, or request IDs into analytics events and does not provide advertising based on an advertising identifier.

Sentry receives only error and fatal events. Ordinary session replay sampling is 0%; only error sessions are eligible. All text and images are masked, and network bodies, headers, screenshots, and view hierarchies are disabled.

8. Automated processing

Questions, translations, scores, and feedback are generated automatically. They are study aids and do not make legal or similarly significant decisions about you.

9. Your rights

You can review, correct, or delete profile data, sharing settings, notification preferences, optional consents, learning records, and your account in the app. You may withdraw marketing consent at any time. Subscription changes, cancellation, refund requests, and purchase restoration can be started from the Membership and Billing screen through management interfaces provided by Apple or RevenueCat.

Requests for access, correction, deletion, restriction, or withdrawal can also be sent to the email below. Identity verification may be required.

10. Deletion

On account withdrawal, access and sessions are disabled immediately. A recoverable asynchronous workflow deletes profile, learning, questions, answers, records, statistics, comments, likes, reports, notifications, terms records, and device links from the active database. Transaction records needed for payment, refund, disputes, or legal obligations are segregated and deleted after the applicable retention period. Failed work is retried and recorded for operations review.

Backup copies are not restored for ordinary use and expire within the 14-day rotation.

11. Security

  • TLS, password hashing, and no plaintext client-secret storage
  • AWS Secrets Manager and least-privilege operational access
  • Cloudflare network protection, administrator authentication, and VPN-based administration
  • Credential masking, seven-day log retention, access and audit records
  • MySQL integrity constraints, Redis AOF/RDB, transactional outbox/inbox, and idempotent event handling
  • Text and image masking for error-session replay

12. Children

BuddyStudy is not directed to children under 14. Contact us to request deletion if a child's data may have been processed.
