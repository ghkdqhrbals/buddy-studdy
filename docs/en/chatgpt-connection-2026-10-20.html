BuddyStudy · ChatGPT learning connection notice

October 20, 2026 · Optional feature

Purpose and shared information

After your separate consent, your connected ChatGPT can retrieve your own studies and topics, questions and hints, answers, scores, feedback and explanations, topic statistics and learning trends to help you review and analyze your learning. Results can include private learning content. This read-only connection excludes API keys, passwords, resumes, personal interest profiles and other users’ records.

Recipient and your choice

The connected service is OpenAI’s ChatGPT. Retrieved content is sent to OpenAI and processed under its privacy policy and the data settings of your ChatGPT account or workspace. Processing may take place outside your country. Connecting is optional; you can continue using BuddyStudy without it.

Identity and permissions

Verify your identity with a code sent to the account email shown in your app profile. Complete verification within 10 minutes; at most five code attempts are allowed. An email shared by multiple accounts cannot connect. learning:read permits access to your own records. offline_access permits renewing the connection without signing in each time. Creating, editing and deleting content are not allowed.

Retention and disconnecting

Access tokens last up to 15 minutes; refresh authorization lasts up to 30 days from initial consent. We store hashes of tokens, codes and browser secrets, account and client identifiers, scopes, consent version and time, and expiration metadata. Expired connections and rotation evidence are periodically deleted and are deleted with your account. Hashed rate-limit keys remain in Redis for up to one hour. Backups follow the existing privacy policy.

Withdrawal and previously shared content

After identity verification, you can disconnect through BuddyStudy’s connection management page to immediately revoke server access. Disconnecting does not delete content already delivered to a ChatGPT conversation. Manage that content separately using ChatGPT’s conversation and data controls.
