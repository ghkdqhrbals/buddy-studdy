Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Overview

BuddyStuddy is a free study app that uses your own OpenAI API key to generate study questions and grade answers. Core study features can be used without a separate app account. The app is designed to keep study records and settings local-first, and uses the backend where needed for AI routing, scheduling, synchronization, push delivery, and signed-in community features.

If you enable question visibility and create a question, signed-in users may see limited public information such as question text, topic, difficulty, creation time, and your public profile. Answers, hints, scores, feedback, explanations, OpenAI API keys, APNs tokens, and device credentials are not shown in public question lists.

BuddyStuddy does not sell personal data and does not intentionally collect government identifiers or sensitive personal information.

Information Stored By The App

BuddyStuddy may store the following information on your device:

  • Study topic, level, prompt, interval, language, notification, record retention, question visibility, and app settings.
  • Your OpenAI API key, if you enter one.
  • Study questions, answer drafts, graded answers, scores, feedback, explanations, and topic statistics.
  • Backend device ID, hashed client secret, APNs device token, platform, APNs environment, app language, and timezone when backend features are used.
  • If you use Google Login for community features: Google account subject, email, display name, avatar URL, and the public bio you enter.
  • If you report a community question: reported question ID, reason, optional message, reporter device ID, and submission time.

Community Profiles And Reports

Community questions are available only after Google Login. Your public profile may show your display name, avatar, and bio to other signed-in users when they view a question you chose to make public. Your email address and Google account identifier are used for account linking and are not shown in public question lists.

When you report a community question, BuddyStuddy stores the report and may forward it to the operator email address for moderation. Reports are used only for abuse review and service operation.

OpenAI API

When you generate a question or grade an answer, BuddyStuddy sends the required study text to OpenAI using the API key you provide. OpenAI processes that information under OpenAI's own terms and privacy practices. Your OpenAI API usage and billing are managed through your own OpenAI account.

BuddyStuddy does not provide an OpenAI account or API key for normal use.

Backend Storage of OpenAI API Key

Your OpenAI API key is a credential, so BuddyStuddy treats it as sensitive. The backend may store it when needed for AI requests, scheduled push delivery, or backend synchronization.

  • On the backend, the key is stored as an encrypted value, never as plain text.
  • Encryption uses AES-256-GCM with an internal server master key (BACKEND_MASTER_KEY).
  • Backend storage is limited to the app features that need server-side AI routing, synchronization, or scheduled push delivery.
  • BuddyStuddy does not expose the decrypted key in API responses or application logs.
  • When you clear the key in settings or disable scheduling, backend usage stops immediately and no new key sync is made.

If you prefer not to use server-side AI features, do not enter an OpenAI API key or disable the relevant backend features in the app.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, BuddyStuddy may schedule local notifications or receive server-scheduled push notifications to remind you about study questions. You can manage notification permission in iOS Settings.

For server-scheduled push delivery, BuddyStuddy may store an APNs device token, study schedule, notification preferences, and an encrypted OpenAI API key on the BuddyStuddy backend. This backend is used to generate and deliver scheduled study-question notifications and keep backend records in sync.

Data Sharing

BuddyStuddy does not sell your personal data. The app may send data to OpenAI when you use AI question generation or grading, may use Google Login for community identity, may use the BuddyStuddy backend for AI routing, settings/records sync, scheduled notification delivery, community profiles, public questions, and reports, and may use Amazon Web Services for backend infrastructure.

OpenAI, Apple Push Notification service, Google Login, and Amazon Web Services may process data in countries where they operate, including the United States. Transfers occur over encrypted connections when you use the relevant feature, and each provider's own terms and privacy practices apply.

Retention And Deletion

You can delete study records in the app. Deleted backend records are immediately removed together with related public-question and report references. Deleting the app removes local app data from the device according to iOS behavior.

When you remove or change the OpenAI API key from app settings, the key is replaced, no longer used, or removed where it is no longer needed for service operation. Deleting your account immediately removes the community profile, sign-in mapping, public questions, and related study records for that account.

Your Choices And Rights

You can view and delete study records in the app, disable question visibility, change notification permission in iOS Settings, and change or remove your OpenAI API key in the app. For access, correction, deletion, suspension, public-question removal, or community profile deletion requests related to backend data, contact the developer by email.

Children

BuddyStuddy is not directed to children under 14. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

Automated Decisions

Grading and feedback are AI-generated study aids. They are not used for decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects. If you need an explanation of a grading result or AI feedback, contact the developer by email.
