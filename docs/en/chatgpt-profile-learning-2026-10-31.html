BuddyStudy ChatGPT learning connection

October 31, 2026

Purpose and data

This optional connection lets you create studies and topics, request questions, submit answers for grading and manage your own learning. It can retrieve studies, topics, questions, hints, answers, scores, feedback, explanations, topic statistics and voice learning history. Results may contain private learning content. API keys, passwords and other users’ records are excluded. Profile, saved resume and interests require the separate profile:read permission described below.

Permissions

learning:read permits learning retrieval. learning:write permits creating and updating studies and topics, requesting and skipping questions, submitting answers for grading, follow-ups and saving custom questions. learning:delete permits deletion of specifically confirmed owned studies and records. Deleted data cannot be restored. Only the scopes shown at consent are granted. Existing read-only connections require fresh consent for expanded access.

Account limits

Question generation uses your existing BuddyStudy monthly allowance (30 on the free tier); reads and answer grading do not consume that generation allowance. Study and topic creation does not consume question quota. This connection does not process payments or subscription changes.

Sharing with OpenAI

Results used in the connected ChatGPT conversation are sent to OpenAI and processed under OpenAI’s privacy policy and your account data settings, possibly overseas. Connecting is optional and the iOS app remains available without it.

Authentication and retention

Verify with the code sent to your account email within 10 minutes, with up to five attempts, or use the existing password of an email-registered account. Password sign-in is rate-limited and never creates an account. Access tokens last up to 15 minutes and refresh authorization lasts up to 30 days from initial consent. The server stores hashes of tokens and codes, account and client identifiers, scopes, consent version and time, and expiration data. Expired grants are periodically removed and removed on account withdrawal. Rate-limit hashes last up to one hour. Backups follow the privacy policy.

Disconnect

Disconnect after verifying your identity in connection management to immediately stop future reads and writes. Content already sent to ChatGPT must be managed separately using ChatGPT conversation and data controls.

Profile access and allowance

profile:read separately authorizes your profile, email, bio, avatar, account status, login provider, sharing preference, saved resume and interests. Existing learning grants never acquire this scope through refresh; a new login and consent are required. Generation uses the same monthly account allowance (30 on the free tier). Reads and grading do not consume generation allowance. Quota exhaustion is an entitlement error; no checkout or upgrade promotion is provided. API keys, passwords and other users’ records remain excluded.
